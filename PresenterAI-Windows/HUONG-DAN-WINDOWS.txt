Yeu cau: Windows 10/11 64-bit, Internet va OpenAI API key dang hoat dong.

1. Giai nen TOAN BO file ZIP va giu nguyen ten thu muc "SA Cook Assistant-win32-x64".
2. Mo "SA Cook Assistant.exe" trong thu muc vua giai nen.
3. Bam "AI key...", nhap key, roi bam "Luu & kiem tra".
4. Chon "Am thanh he thong" de nghe video/cuoc goi, hoac "Microphone".
5. Bam "Bat dau nghe". AUTO tu nhan dien; SPACE chi chot khi bam phim Space hoac nut "Chot cau".
6. Dung nut A-/A+ va 3 muc do nen trong menu "..." de doc de hon.
7. Bam "Update" de app tu tai, xac minh, thay ban moi va mo lai.
