# Presenter AI v5.20

- Returns only ready-to-speak English answers, not suggestions about how to answer.
- Uses the presenter's first-person voice (`I/my`, or `we/our`) for actions, experience, choices, and opinions.
- States factual definitions directly without unnecessary framing.
- Limits a normal answer to 1–3 short sentences and 45 words; multipart answers stay under 90 words.
- Reduces output-token budgets for faster responses.
- Starts a new answer-cache generation so older verbose answers are not reused.
